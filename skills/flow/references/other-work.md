# Other Deliverables

For research, define the question and the required confidence, consult appropriate primary sources, and separate reported facts from inference. Track dates when the answer can change. An unanswered subquestion is a limitation, not a reason to invent evidence.

For documents, presentations, data analysis, or design, identify the audience, source artifacts, intended use, and acceptance criteria. Reuse existing artifact tools or project-local skills. Verify the actual deliverable: inspect rendered pages, recalculate formulas, check units and provenance, or exercise the intended interaction as applicable.

For repeatable operational work, define input, result, checkpoints, and recovery. Distinguish a failed operation from an uncertain outcome before retrying. Apply authorization at the actual external action rather than inferring it from permission to prepare the artifact.

Deliver the requested artifact with a concise statement of what changed and what was checked. Keep reusable knowledge in the appropriate project library and record only enough handoff context to resume correctly.
